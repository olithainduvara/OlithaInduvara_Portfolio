Second Place — Idea to Invention Competition 2025

17 January 2026 Oak Ray Resort, Kandy UPAA–USA & Faculty of Engineering, UoP

Team FloodLink proudly secured 2nd Place at the inaugural Idea to Invention (I-TO-I) Competition under the UPAA–USA Innovation Award.

FloodLink — a real-time flood-monitoring and prediction system powered by LoRa networks — proudly secured Second Place at the inaugural Idea to Invention (I-TO-I) Competition under the UPAA–USA Innovation Award.

The certificate awarding ceremony was organised by the University of Peradeniya Alumni Association – USA (UPAA–USA) and the Faculty of Engineering, University of Peradeniya — a milestone moment in a journey driven by innovation and impact.

Citizen Science SEDS Sri Lanka Astrometrica

International Asteroid Search Campaign

Astrometrica · Pan-STARRS telescope data 5 campaigns · 4 preliminary asteroids

Across five International Asteroid Search Campaigns organised by SEDS Sri Lanka, our team analysed real Pan-STARRS telescope data to identify potential new asteroids — discovering four preliminary asteroids.

Had the amazing opportunity to participate in the International Asteroid Search Campaign, where we analysed telescope data using Astrometrica software to identify potential new asteroids.

The campaigns involved analysing high-quality sky-survey images captured by the Pan-STARRS telescope in Hawaii. Using Astrometrica, our team carefully examined each frame to identify potential Near-Earth Objects (NEOs) and main-belt asteroids — performing precise detection and verification, analysing datasets with high accuracy, and contributing to global efforts in tracking potentially hazardous objects and planetary defense.
