🎯 Leadership President, Buddhist Society FOE Dharma Deshanmaya Pinkama

Leadership & Event Coordinating

Leading the Dharma Deshanmaya Pinkama as President of the Buddhist Society of the Faculty of Engineering — uniting all faculty Buddhist societies and broadcasting the event live worldwide with Shraddha TV.

Leading the “Dharma Deshanmaya Pinkama” as the President of the Buddhist Society of the Faculty of Engineering has been a profound milestone in my leadership journey. Organizing one of the largest Buddhist events at the University of Peradeniya required extensive event management, strategic planning, and cross-faculty coordination. I am incredibly proud that we successfully united and collaborated with all the faculty Buddhist societies across the university to make this massive undertaking a reality.

To elevate the event even further, we made university history by partnering with the Shraddha TV media network to broadcast this spiritual program live from the WUS premises to a worldwide audience. A huge thank you to my dedicated team and everyone who supported this historic collaboration! 🙏✨
