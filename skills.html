Technical Skill Hardware & Soldering Circuit Design

Electronics Enthusiast & Hardware Soldering

An electronics enthusiast at heart — from custom perfboards to precision soldering, I bring circuits to life for every hardware project I take on.

I'm an electronics enthusiast at heart, and hardware is where I feel most at home. Beyond writing firmware, I enjoy designing circuits, hand-soldering components and turning a bare perfboard into a working, field-ready system — a practical skill I've built up across nearly every hardware project I've been part of.

For FloodLink, I was responsible for the complete circuit assembly and soldering of the LoRa-based sensor nodes — interfacing the ultrasonic sensors, LoRa transceiver modules and microcontrollers, and hand-wiring every connection on the field-deployable boards myself.

On SkyAQ, our air-quality measurement drone, I assembled and soldered the core sensor stack — the ESP32 controller, PM2.5 sensor, GPS module and SD-card interface — into a single reliable circuit built to survive flight vibration and handling.

I also designed and built a compact EMG analog front-end for one of our research projects, hand-soldering the instrumentation-amplifier stage directly along clean lead paths with barely any jumper wires — a neat, low-noise layout built for signal integrity.

Key Highlights

Hand-soldered every FloodLink sensor node from scratch
Assembled the ESP32 & sensor stack for SkyAQ
Designed and soldered a custom EMG analog front-end
Clean, minimal-wire soldering with neat lead-path routing
Comfortable reading schematics and hand-wiring perfboards
Strong practical grounding in circuit design & debugging

Public Speaking & Presenting

Pandith W. D. Amaradewa Auditorium, Wayamba University

Standing in front of hundreds of students to speak on AI, technology and the future of robotics — public speaking and presenting is a skill I've actively built outside the classroom.

I was invited by the Lions Club of Puttalam Division to conduct a session on AI, Technology and Future Robotics for students who had just finished their G.C.E. Ordinary Level examinations, aimed at building their interest in these fields before they choose their next academic path.

The session drew a large turnout, with many students attending to learn about where technology is heading and how AI and robotics are shaping the future. Presenting to such a large, curious audience pushed me to sharpen how I structure a talk, hold attention and explain technical ideas in a way that's engaging and easy to follow.

Opportunities like this have become one of the main ways I've developed my public speaking and presenting skills — a skill set that complements my engineering background, and one I continue to look for opportunities to grow through community and academic events.

Leadership & Event Coordinating

Leading the Dharma Deshanmaya Pinkama as President of the Buddhist Society of the Faculty of Engineering — uniting all faculty Buddhist societies and broadcasting the event live worldwide with Shraddha TV.

Leading the “Dharma Deshanmaya Pinkama” as the President of the Buddhist Society of the Faculty of Engineering has been a profound milestone in my leadership journey. Organizing one of the largest Buddhist events at the University of Peradeniya required extensive event management, strategic planning, and cross-faculty coordination.
