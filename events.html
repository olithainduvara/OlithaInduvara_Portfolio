Spaghetti Tower Competition — SLIIT Tower Fest

Structural design challenge SLIIT Campus

Part of a team that designed a spaghetti tower that impressively supported 40.18 kg at the SLIIT Tower Fest.

Excited to share our achievement at the Spaghetti Tower Competition during the SLIIT Tower Fest! Our tower impressively supported 40.18 kg, showcasing a design that combined strength, stability and creativity.

Key Highlights

Twisted spaghetti strands to enhance lateral stiffness and delay failure, adding resilience under load
Internal bracing to minimise buckling and deflection while ensuring even load distribution
Staged construction techniques to prevent premature buckling during assembly
Thicker column sections to improve overall stiffness and structural stability
Spaghetti Tower SLIIT Tower Fest Structural Design Teamwork 40.18 kg

Media Media Production EngEx 2025

EngEx 2025 — Media Production & the First University Drone Show

Handled media production for EngEx 2025 — Sri Lanka's largest engineering exhibition — with content shown across 140+ LED walls and the first-ever university drone show.

Proud to have handled the media production for EngEx 2025. My videos were showcased across 140+ LED walls throughout Sri Lanka and shared widely on social media, and I had the privilege of capturing the drone show — the first ever in university history — through aerial cinematography.

The media team powered the story of the exhibition: 10,000+ shutters capturing milestones, 25+ interviews giving engineers a voice, more than two hours of drone flight time, and rapid edits, live coverage and end-of-day recap reels so EngEx could shine brighter. It was unforgettable to see creativity and technology come together on such a grand scale.
